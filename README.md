# File Naming Convention

 Please follow the naming convention below when naming your files:

```
XX-PC-Software-FirstName-LastName
```

 ## Naming Format

 - **XX** — Your **roll number**.
- **PC-Software** — The name of the subject/course.
- **FirstName** — Your **first name according to your official name**.
- **LastName** — Your **last name according to your official name**.

 ## Example

 If your roll number is `58`, your subject is `PC-Software`, and your official name is **Pratham Jaiswal**, your file should be named:

```
58-C-Language-Pratham-Jaiswal
```

 ## Important

 - Use your **roll number** in place of `XX`.
- Enter your **First Name and Last Name exactly as they appear in your official records**.
- Do not use nicknames, short forms, or unofficial names.
- Follow the format consistently to ensure proper identification and organization of submissions.
